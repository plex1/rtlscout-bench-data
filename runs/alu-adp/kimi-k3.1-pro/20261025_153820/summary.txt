- **Best approach:** Keep the upstream shared datapaths in `starting_point.sv` and make a small, targeted change: bypass the rounding adder when `adder_round_value == 0`. This passed all **1,028,192 checks** and achieved the best cost: **597,187 area·delay**, with **437.20 area** and **1365.95 ps delay**. Versus the original **645,629**, this is about a **7.5% improvement**.
- **Most impactful optimization:** Avoiding the second 32-bit carry chain for non-rounding ADD/SUB operations improved both area and delay. The default **500 ps** synthesis constraint also produced the best product; relaxed constraints generally increased delay more than they reduced area.
- **What regressed:** The clean rewrite in `design.sv` was functionally correct but cost **992,051**, due to duplicated arithmetic and muxing. Other regressions included direct shuffle equations, hierarchical result decoding, grouped opcode decoding, explicit lane adders, a Sklansky prefix adder, clip-comparison rewriting, divider reset/zero-detect changes, and zero-shift normalization bypasses. These often looked smaller in RTL but mapped worse or lengthened the critical path.
- **Lessons for next time:** The upstream shared shifter, adder, and mux structures are already efficient; preserve them unless a change is measured independently. Save an exact file copy whenever a new best is found, since manual restoration can alter synthesis results. Future work should focus on the remaining add/normalize/result-mux critical path, using one isolated change per evaluation and retaining exact best checkpoints.
