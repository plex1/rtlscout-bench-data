## Summary

### 1. Approaches tried
- **Kept/reused the provided `starting_point.sv`** as the first baseline. It passed with cost **645,629** (`area_delay_product`).
- **Attempted a from-scratch behavioural rewrite (`design_simple.sv`)** using functions and `always_comb`, keeping the original serial-divider submodule.
- **Attempted small structural edits to `starting_point.sv`**, specifically simplifying the 32-bit comparison path.

### 2. Best result
- The best result remained the **baseline unmodified `starting_point.sv`**:
  - Correctness: **PASS**
  - Cost: **645,629 area_delay_product**
  - Delay: **~1,462 ps**
  - Area: **~441.5**

No later variant improved on this.

### 3. What worked / didn't work

#### What worked
- The original CV32E40P ALU structure is already quite good for the evaluated metric. Its shared adder/shifter/comparator architecture was better than my simplified synthesis-generated RTL.
- Saving the originally working file by not collapsing the comparison cascade into direct 32-bit comparisons was important for area.

#### What caused regressions
- **`design_simple.sv`** eventually became functionally correct, but its area/delay cost blew up to **~1,098,474** (area ~661, delay ~1,661 ps). The large procedural case/function synthesis created a worse critical path and significantly larger area.
- Initial `always_comb` latches from incomplete vector assignments in loops caused both simulation and synthesis failures.
- A subtle shift amount indexing bug in vector modes produced incorrect results: extracting the amount from the wrong bit slice. It was fixed after analyzing stimulus errors.
- Modifying `starting_point.sv` to use direct 32-bit comparisons for scalar compare mode raised cost to **~667,421** rather than reducing it, despite the logic appearing simpler.

### 4. Lessons learned
- The provided upstream RTL is already highly optimized for this metric; small “simplifications” can hurt area-delay product even if they remain functionally correct.
- Synthesis evaluates structural patterns, not just operator count. A structured shared datapath often beats a large flat `case`/`function` implementation.
- Moving to a behavioural style with loops introduced latch risk and produced larger/slower logic in Yosys. The original generate-based structural style was better for PPA.
- Shift-amount lane indexing and signed/unsigned shift conversions were the most error-prone details when rewriting the ALU.

### Files
- **`starting_point.sv`**: baseline/best; currently includes a non-beneficial experimental comparison edit that should be reverted to the original upstream version.
- **`design_simple.sv`**: functional but much worse cost (~1.098M), not recommended.
