3. **What didn’t work / caused regressions**

     - Correctness failures in ABS/CLIP/vector comparison and divider handshake timing.
     - Never reached a successful PPA evaluation.

     - Final evaluation still failed with `ready_o` mismatch on DIVU and wrong division results.

4. **Lessons learned**
   - The original shared adder/shifter/divider datapath is cycle-critical and difficult to replace without exact equivalence of the divider’s `OpB_DI` / `div_shift` logic.
   - Function syntax with returned replication or indexed part-selects is fragile across the tools. Prefer explicit continuous assignments and simple helper functions.
   - Start from the known-good file and make smaller, deliberately targeted edits, not wholesale rewrites.
   - Spending many steps on a direct rewrite that did not pass left no chance to exploit small area-delay optimizations on the passing baseline.
   - Next time would keep `starting_point.sv` identical for divider/bit-count logic and only optimize isolated combinational paths, validating each incremental edit immediately.
