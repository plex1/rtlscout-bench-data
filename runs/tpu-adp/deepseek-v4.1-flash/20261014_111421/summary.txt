### 2. Highest-impact optimizations
1. **Registering the product inside the PE** (splitting multiply from accumulate into separate stages) — the 8×8 multiply alone is ~500 ps, so isolating it was the single biggest win. Latency was preserved for free by making the bottom row's final add combinational (the `psum_v[N]` net is already registered).
2. **Splitting the 8×8 multiply into two 4×8 multiplies** (`a = ah*16 + al`; exact because `a*w = (ah*w)*16 + al*w`).
3. **Narrowing accumulators 32→19 bits** (true max |Σ| = 8·128·128 = 131072 < 2¹⁸) — pure area win, bit-identical output.
4. Carry-save accumulation — helps, but is second-order once the multiplier is pipelined.

### 3. What didn't work
- `design_flat2.sv`/`design.sv`: both flat models fail **identically at line 271** (21/516) in the mid-drain weight-reload region — hard evidence the flat `C[m][j]=Σ A[m][i]·W[k][j]` model is **not** bit-exact. The systolic PE mesh is *required*; don't retry a flat rewrite.
- Hand-written Booth and Kogge-Stone were both **slower** than Yosys's own `*` / `+` mapping.
- `target_delay` is a **dead knob** here: 200 and 3000 both produced essentially the same delay/area.
- Several self-inflicted bugs from rewriting whole files: prefix carry misalignment (`{1'b0,g[W-2:0]}` vs `{g[W-2:0],1'b0}`), product width arithmetic (`DW/2+3` = 7 bits, not 12), delay-line depth off by one when adding an output register, and deleting the bottom row's `a_east` shift register.

### 4. Lessons / next time
- **Smallest correct change first.** Multiplier width, prefix encodings, and delay-line depths were the recurring bug sources; use `edit_file` on a passing file rather than `create_file` from scratch.
- Keep the **systolic mesh**; it is load-bearing for bit-exactness. Optimize *inside* the PE and inside the skew/de-skew chains.
- Don't hand-roll arithmetic operators — Yosys beats them.
- Next steps worth trying: evaluate `design_p4.sv` (split-mult + CSA, expected ~<2.6e6, ~520 ps); then consider CSD/shift-add decomposition of the weights, or splitting the skew/de-skew shift registers into parallel banks.
