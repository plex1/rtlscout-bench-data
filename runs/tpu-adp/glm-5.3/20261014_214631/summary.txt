### Lessons
- The critical structural insight was that only the *product cycle* is pinned (weight-snapshot semantics); everything else (representation, adder structure, tree shape) is free.
- Yosys's inferred multiplier/adder carry-propagate logic is the delay bottleneck; replacing it with explicit CSA trees was worth ~170 ps at no area cost.
- Register enable muxes (`if (en)`) sit on every critical path â a real but unrecoverable ~20-40 ps cost here given the reload semantics.
