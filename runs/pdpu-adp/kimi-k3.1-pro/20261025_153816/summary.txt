Work was done in `starting_point.v`. The best passing result was **737,804.96 area-delay product**, down from **968,044.66** baseline (**23.8% improvement**), with all **1,999,992 checks passing**.

1. **Approaches that worked**
   - Replaced general 4×4 Booth mantissa multipliers with direct unsigned multiplication.
   - Removed redundant carry-save product registers and final product additions.
   - Simplified signed maximum comparators.
   - Deferred two’s-complement increments: registered conditional complements and added a population count of negative terms later.
   - Replaced the recursive accumulation compressor tree with a balanced modular adder tree.
   - Specialized the posit(8,2) decoder’s regime shifting while retaining the generic accumulator decoder.

2. **Largest improvements**
   - Deferred negation plus consolidated correction: roughly **10% cost reduction**.
   - Balanced accumulation tree: approximately **5% further reduction**.
   - Direct multiplication and simplified posit(8,2) decode shifting provided additional area and delay gains.

3. **Regressions and failures**
   - Moving the complete maximum tree before pipe1, or merging normalization/encoding before pipe5, created much longer critical paths.
   - A collapsed encoder shift was initially algebraically incorrect; the corrected form still synthesized worse.
   - A custom priority normalizer, 9/8 maximum-tree split, and saturated alignment-distance implementation all passed after correction but mapped worse.
   - Narrowing exponent comparisons broke correctness because the seventh sum bit is required.
   - Timing-target sweeps did not beat the best structural result.

4. **Lessons**
   - Fixed-width specialization and arithmetic identities were more effective than aggressive pipeline repartitioning.
   - Shallower RTL logic does not guarantee better ASAP7 mapping; every structural change needs evaluation.
   - Next time, checkpoint each best variant in a separate file and prioritize fixed-width arithmetic restructuring before extensive timing-constraint experiments.
