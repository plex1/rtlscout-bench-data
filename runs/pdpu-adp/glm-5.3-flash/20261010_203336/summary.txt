### 2. The key algebra (for the next agent)
Two's-complement negate without incrementers — `s*|p| = (s ? ~|p| : |p|) + s (mod 2^w)`:
- Feed each of the 17 items as `(pipe2_signs[y] ? ~product_shifted : product_shifted)` zero-extended to 21 bits — **no incrementer**.
- Add **one popcount row set**: rows = `signs_pop << p` for bit planes p (folds all the `+s_y` terms). **CAUTION: `signs_pop` must be 5 bits (17 items) → needs 5 rows, `.N(22)`;** my last edit used only 4 rows/`.N(21)`, which is wrong for popcount≥16. Verify before evaluating.
- Same identity merges the pipe5 negate: `sum_c = (R ^ {SUM_WIDTH{final_sign}}) + final_sign`, and the encoder round: `result_rnd = (value_before_round ^ {(n-1){sign_i}}) + (sign_i ^ round_value)` — one adder instead of negate-then-add.
